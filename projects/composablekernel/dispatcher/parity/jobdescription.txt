## Key architectural decisions to know

- **Two kinds of name**: registry identifier (canonical, scheduler "default"→"auto") vs kernel/file name (raw TE strings). Swapping them causes silent wrong-kernel dispatch.
- **`_preshuffle` suffix**: te_kernel_name() and sweep_runner._kernel_name() must append `_preshuffle` for pipeline == "preshufflev2". This was Bug #1; it's now in te_kernel_name() but sweep_runner._kernel_name() is untested.
- **preshufflev2 double_buffer**: NOT A BUG — codegen and translator agree (both set True). Documented in §2 of PORTING_DECISIONS.md and tested in TestDoubleBuffer.test_preshufflev2_sets_double_buffer.
- **Performance spec**: _PERF_RUNS=10, perf_tol=0.02, median of 10 outer runs, GPU timer.
- **Skipped combos**: compv1/compv2/preshufflev1 (no codegen path), interwave+compv3 (unsupported trait), split_k>255 (uint8_t overflow).
