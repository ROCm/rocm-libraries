Recommended dependency fixes
- Add tensilelite-qam depends on tensilelite-5ob. Rationale: qam is a slot-state ownership refactor, and its own acceptance says slot lifetime/grouped GEMM offsets/batch-pointer setup touch the active-slot invariant. The report separately identifies grouped-GEMM ring lifecycle as an untested high-risk production contract. Covering grouped ring lifecycle before consolidating slot state would reduce refactor risk.
- Consider adding tensilelite-qam depends on tensilelite-nxb if the intended policy is "production-composition coverage before state-owner refactors." This is a softer recommendation than qam -> 5ob because qam could add its own targeted composition checks, but nxb is the broader real scheduler/listener/DataInitialization proof that would protect the same ring-slot surface.
- I would not add hard dependencies from tensilelite-8en to tensilelite-yn6 or from tensilelite-qfr to tensilelite-nxb. Those beads can be implemented independently even though their tests touch adjacent scheduler/listener paths.
