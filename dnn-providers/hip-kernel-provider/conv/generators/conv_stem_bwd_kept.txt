# order); --universe emits every config it enumerates.

# How this list was chosen (2026-11-12). Every config of the universe that a graph admits was timed
# on that graph (filter transform and main kernel, the fastest of 8 launches after one warm-up,
# which shrugs off interference from other jobs on the node) on gfx950 (MI355X), gfx942 (MI325X),
# gfx90a (MI210), gfx908 (MI100), gfx1201 (R9700), gfx1101 (V710, the gfx11 atoms) and gfx906
# (MI60). Graphs: the 82 conv-kpi corpus stems (backward data, at most 4 input
# channels, one group), and stems of every geometry the universe covers (6x6 / 5x5 / 2x2 stride 2,
# 5x5 / 7x7 stride 1, 1x1, 11x11 stride 4), each in fp32 / fp16 / bf16 and NCHW / NHWC; plus a
# probe of each admission class (1, 3 and 4 input channels; dy rows of whole and of partial
# 16-byte vectors). Configs are visited best first (geometric mean of time over the graph's best,
# over all the graphs they serve); a config is dropped when, on every (architecture, graph) it
# serves, a config already kept is at most 2% slower. So every graph the universe serves is still
# served (the same admission), and no measured graph's best time grows by more than 2%.
