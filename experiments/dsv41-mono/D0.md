# D0: plan of record for DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash on rocKE (W12, TP2 3x goal + TP1)

Decision record after probes P1 (`probes/P1.md`), P2 (`reverse/MONO-STAGES.md`), P3 (`tp1/TP1.md`) and P4
(`wide/P4.md`), with the B0 ratios (`baseline/`). Ratios only. Absolute numbers live in
`hackathon-prep/private-perf/dsv41-mono/`. Nothing was run for this document. Every number is taken or derived from the
files named above; derived numbers show their arithmetic.

"Step" = the best measured TP2 decode step at that CONC (mono at C1-C4, native at C8 / C16). "Floor" = the per-rank
bytes of a step with measured routing, at the achievable HBM read stream (P1 §5, P4 §5.2). "Stall" = the share of a
request's decode window lost to other requests' eager prefills (P1 §3).

## 0. Verdict on the 3x goal (read first)

**With the plan below, 3x per-request tok/s over ATOM's best served TP2 path is not expected at any CONC from 1 to 16.**
The central estimate is 1.5-1.75x, and the top of the range is 2.0-2.4x. The binding limits:

- **C1-C4: bytes.** Today's step is already 2.1-2.4x the floor. Even a perfect step at 85% of the stream reaches only
  2.0-2.3x. 3x needs 1.33-1.48x fewer bytes per accepted token. The candidate byte cuts (WP3) sum to about 1.15-1.4x,
  and most of them are accuracy-gated.
- **C8-C16: bytes plus prefill.** The bytes are 0.84-0.88 routed MoE, and ATOM's MoE already runs at about 1.2x the
  floor. 3x needs the stall at zero, a 1.14-1.19x byte cut, and every latency bucket at its floor. That is the top
  edge of the range, not the centre.
- **C32-C64** (P4, not in the B0 matrix): 3x is inside the top of the estimate (2.3-3.2x) once the prefill stall is
  removed, because the stall there is about half the served step.
- **TP1** runs (P3) and passes the accuracy gate, but it is a capacity route, not a speed route: per request it is
  0.47-0.74x of TP2.

3x stays the floor-style target, and the plan is built to reach for it: the levers that could close the gap
(acceptance length, ragged verify, quantized projections and heads) are WPs, not footnotes. The final matrix reports
the measured ratio per CONC against 3x, with the reason wherever it falls short.

## 1. Per-CONC TP2 budget: what 3x requires

A 3x per-request rate at the same acceptance length (AL) needs (step + stall) per accepted token at 1/3 of today's.
With the stall removed, the 3x step in units of today's step is (1/3) / (1 - stall).

| | C1 | C2 | C4 | C8 | C16 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| floor / step (P1 §7) | 0.42 | 0.47 | 0.47 | 0.45 | 0.45 |
| stall share today | 0 | 0.12 | 0.20 | 0.25 | 0.28 |
| 3x step with stall = 0 (units of today's step) | 0.333 | 0.379 | 0.417 | 0.444 | 0.463 |
| floor at 85% stream (floor / 0.85) | 0.494 | 0.553 | 0.553 | 0.529 | 0.529 |
| byte cut per accepted token needed (row 4 / row 3) | 1.48 | 1.46 | 1.33 | 1.19 | 1.14 |

| **plan central (top)** | **1.6 (2.2)** | **1.65 (2.2)** | **1.5 (2.0)** | **1.75 (2.3)** | **1.75 (2.4)** |

How the "byte cut available" rows are derived (P1 §5 shares of the floor; cuts from P1 §7):

- **WP3a, exact:**
  - shard the replicated tensors across the 2 ranks (wqkv_a, router, mHC, Engram wkv, main_proj). Column split plus
    all-gather computes every output element exactly as today. About -0.04 of the C1 floor, falling to -0.01 at C16.
  - draft vocabulary subset (Qwen N8: output-exact under greedy verify, changes only acceptance). About -0.03 at C1,
    -0.01 at C16.
- **WP3b, accuracy-gated:**
  - attention projections FP8 to MXFP4: half of their 0.19 / 0.09 / 0.07 / 0.05 floor share;
  - target and draft LM heads bf16 to FP8: about -0.05 at C1, -0.02 at C16;
  - shared expert FP8 to MXFP4: -0.025 at C1, -0.005 at C16.
- **WP3c, ragged verify:** the routed share is 0.58 / 0.79 / 0.84 / 0.88 of the floor, and the MoE union U shrinks
  with the active rows. Booked at -0.05..-0.10 of the routed bytes, minus the AL it costs. This is the most uncertain
  line.

**Composition of the plan line** (per-WP central gains from §4, multiplied, positive lines x0.8 as in Qwen D0):
C1 1.08 x 1.18 x 1.22 x 1.08 = 1.68; C8 1.22 x 1.03 x 1.06 x 1.06 x 1.04 x 1.18 = 1.73; the other cells likewise.
The 0.8 discount and the interaction (each WP shrinks the base the next one works on) put the centre at 1.5-1.75. The
top of the range assumes the upper end of every WP and WP3c landing.

## 3. TP1 path

- **Done (P3).** TP1 holds 26 GiB of cold experts plus the embedding in pinned host memory over UVA
  (`ATOM_DSV41_RK_COLD`, `EMBED_HOST`). It runs native + DSpark, because ATOM's mono refuses TP1. It passes the split
  numerics gate and GSM8K/passkey. Strict-match is 0.007 under the lower TP2 twin: that passes the binomial rule but
  sits outside the literal twin range, and the note says so.
- **WP7 (below).** Make the plan's layer kernels serve at tp=1, then fix TP1's known costs:
  - no mono;
  - untuned aiter MoE for TP1 shapes (the floor shows 0.88-0.93 is reachable from 24 rows);
  - the two-call cold split (up to about 5%).
- **Side arm.** A DP2 arm (two TP1 replicas on the same two GPUs) at C >= 16 is reported next to TP2 for information.
  It is not TP2, so it does not count toward the TP2 target. The Qwen lesson applies: match GPU counts.

### WP1: prefill off the decode critical path (runtime)
- **What.** Three levers, one knob each, combinable:
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_PREFILL_BATCH=<n,ms>`: admit new requests together, so one eager prefill serves several requests
    and its host launch cost is amortised;
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_PREFILL_GRAPH=<len buckets>`: graph-capture the small-prefill path at bucketed lengths. Today the
    span is about 4.5x the lagging rank's GPU work, and the other rank waits 0.70 of it inside all-reduce;
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_PREFILL_MIX=<rows>`: chunked prefill rows piggy-backed on the decode step, up to the step's width.
- **Gates.**
  - Prefill outputs bitwise vs eager for BATCH (same kernels, per-request rows) and GRAPH.
  - MIX is tolerance-class (it changes the GEMM shapes): accuracy gate.
  - Mutants: a wrong prefill length bucket and a dropped KV page must fail the gate.
  - The P1 stall metric (HOSTTL / KMARK) must fall at C2-C16; the decode-only step must not regress beyond twin noise.
- **Expected.** The stall falls 0.12 / 0.20 / 0.25 / 0.28 to 0.04-0.08. The per-request gains are in the table.
  At C32 / C64 (P4) this is the largest single item.

### WP2: device-side step metadata, the eager tail into the graph, a persistent tail launch
- **What.**
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_DMETA`: the next step's positions, slot mapping, verify width and build choice are computed on the
    device by the tail and consumed by the next graph replay. No host `prepare` on the critical path; ATOM's
    `step_begin` zero + fence is replaced by epoch tags.
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_TAIL`: one persistent tail launch inside the graph. It runs the target head argmax, verify/accept,
    draft-KV write, sampler, draft backbone hand-off, draft head + Markov (with pair-form argmax merges from WP5) and
    the meta.
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_MSTEP=<k>`: replay k decode steps per host round-trip, falling back to the host on finish or admit.
  - Qwen pieces reused: P9 verify tail, round-boundary metadata (TRIM + ADV), the K=0 fused-argmax tail.
- **Gates.**
  - Token streams bitwise vs B0 on GSM8K-64 at C1 / C4 under greedy (the tail must reproduce ATOM's sampler exactly).
    Because the served path is not run-to-run deterministic (P2: 0.28 identical), the bitwise check runs the offline
    engine with fixed batch composition.
  - Mutants: off-by-one position, stale verify width, skipped KV commit.
  - A launch-fatal word is checked per replay (Qwen D0.8 amendment).
- **Expected.** Eager tail 0.013-0.035 and meta 0.010-0.022 of the step go to near 0. Host work leaves the critical path.
  - The draft head/Markov, 0.009-0.032 of the step with 5 sequential all-gathers, shrinks to about half.
  - Required for any C1 result above about 2x.

### WP3: bytes per accepted token
- **WP3a (exact).**
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_SHARD`: column-shard the replicated wqkv_a, router, mHC, Engram wkv and main_proj, then all-gather.
    Bitwise gate.
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_DVOCAB=<k>`: draft vocabulary subset (Qwen N8). Output-exact under greedy; gate on AL and on
    identical target tokens in the offline engine.
- **WP3b (accuracy-gated).**
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_QPROJ=mxfp4`: attention projections wq_b, wo_a, wo_b;
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_QHEAD=fp8`: target and draft heads;
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_QSHARED=mxfp4`: shared expert.
  - Each is gated alone, then the union. Mutants: a scale-byte flip and transposed blocks must fail.
- **WP3c (acceptance-sensitive).**
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_RAGGED`: per-request verify width from the DSpark confidence head, written to the device meta (needs
    WP2). The MoE routes active rows only, so the union U shrinks.
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_DLEN=<n>`: draft length 4-7 as a knob, to measure AL against verify bytes.
  - The metric is bytes per accepted token, measured, not modelled. AL is measured on GSM8K plus a non-GSM8K prompt
    set, because routing is GSM8K-only in P4.
- **Expected.** Byte cuts per §1, realised at about 0.8 of their floor share. C1 1.18 (1.08 exact-only to 1.35 with
  everything), C16 1.05.

### WP4: small-batch rocKE stage ports with the Qwen levers (rows <= 48)
- **What, in P2's order.**
  1. `ATOM_DSV41_RK_K2SPLIT`: split K2 by layer kind (indexer / non-indexer / layer 0). Numerics-free; removes the
     SGPR spills from the ~30 layers that never index.
  2. The FP8 GEMV family (wq_b, wo_a, wo_b, shared) on the `stage_wqkv` core, with the `sop` and `ra` levers. They tied
     on K1's single GEMV but belong on K2's many-MFMA stages.
  3. The K2b MXFP4 MoE port, including the 35-of-42 scale rewrites.
  4. Fold compressor, candidate table, Engram and the per-layer meta into the layer launch (D0.1).
  5. Qwen schedule levers: NUMA dual copies, holds, spread, XCD homing (expert to XCD by id hash), prepoll, epoch tags
     instead of the per-step mailbox zero.
  6. Row-scaled attention core above 12 rows (tolerance knob).
  7. Rust direct backend as per-kernel auto-select.
  - Knobs: `ATOM_DSV41_RK_EXTERN=<stage list>` (exists, from P2), `ATOM_DSV41_RK_SCHED=<Qwen sched grammar>`.
- **Gates.**
  - Bitwise vs ATOM's K1/K2 over 3 seeds at 1 / 6 / 12 / 24 / 48 rows, on both nodes.
  - K2 runs in a 2-GPU rig, because peers receive its partials.
  - Knob-off `.ll` / `.text` identical to df86c482.
  - P2's mutants extended to K2: `put_bit`, `chain_drop`, `row`, `scale_byte`, `opsel`, plus `peer_order` and `tag_epoch`.
  - Tuner with lookback over every lever (surrogate + uncertainty).
- **Expected.** The latency buckets (mHC, router, indexer, attention core, shared, attention projections) move toward
  the floor. C1 1.22, C8 1.06, which turns the 48-row tie into a win.

### WP5: in-kernel CCL for the TP2 reductions
- **What.** `ATOM_DSV41_RK_CCL=<form>`:
  - epoch-tagged lamport / pair forms for the wo_b and MoE-down partials (no per-step zero, no `step_begin` fence);
  - NUMA homing of receive buffers;
  - tile-flag overlap, so the consumer starts on landed tiles;
  - pair-form argmax merges for the head and the 5 Markov steps;
  - CCL-LM (`qwen38_mono/ccl` `lm_select`) for the wide all-reduces at 480 KiB-3.75 MiB;
  - `ar_dtype=fp8` as a gated knob.
- **Gates.**
  - Bitwise reduction order vs ATOM's rank-ordered sum, at 1-48 rows (mono) and 48-384 rows (wide).
  - Mutants: swapped rank order, stale epoch, dropped tile flag.
  - Timing vs ATOM's in-kernel exchange (small) and the aiter all-reduce (wide), with the CCL A-vs-A twin.
  - Re-derive every win from raw logs (the Qwen 3x-inflated RCCL claim).
- **Expected.** The mono C1 CCL bucket is 0.19 ± 0.1 of the step (rank skew inflates it), so 1.03-1.15. Wide gains come
  from overlap only: P4 found replacement alone is worth under 0.02. C16 1.05, more at C32 / C64 where the all-reduce is
  0.16-0.22 of the step.

### WP6: wide path for C >= 8 (rows > 48)
- **What.**
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_WIDE=<row buckets>`: rocKE large-batch MoE ported from `moe_lb2`. It folds top-k, sort and quant into
    one kernel (P4: 0.05-0.12 of the step), uses `fnext` combine into the next stage, and streams only the touched
    experts.
  - E1-style persistent FP8 dense GEMMs (0.07-0.09 of the step).
  - Fused mHC / norm / RoPE seams (the small-kernel tail, 0.13-0.25).
  - Width-specialised builds chosen on the device (D0.2, using WP2's meta).
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_LADDER`: exact capture rungs 10/12/14.
  - MAX_REQUESTS lifted for the draft through the same builds.
- **Gates.**
  - Tolerance-class: a layer numerics gate vs ATOM's native layer at 48 / 96 / 192 rows (P3-style threshold), then the
    GSM8K + passkey accuracy gate.
  - The MoE coverage gate from P4's `moe_floor`.
  - Mutants: a skipped expert, a wrong segment order in `fnext`, a wrong top-k tie-break.
  - Timing vs ATOM's `Mxfp4MoEMethod` at pad 128 (rule 249: hold the routing tensors alive under graphs).
- **Expected.** C8 1.18, C16 1.22 on the step. A 1.0x MoE floor is not booked: Qwen's `moe_lb2` reached 0.72-0.78 of
  floor speed, so the prize is the fold and the seams.

### WP7: TP1 serving on the stack
- **What.**
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_TP1MONO`: the WP4 / WP2 kernels at tp=1 (CCL becomes a no-op; MAX_ROWS as WP6).
  - aiter MoE configs tuned for the TP1 shapes.
  - `ATOM_DSV41_RK_COLD_FUSED`: one masked MoE call with host-resident cold experts in place of P3's two calls.
  - A DP2 information arm.
- **Gates.**
  - P3's split numerics gate and mutants (`perm`, `zero`, `permall`), plus the accuracy gate vs P3's TP1.
- **Expected.** 1.2-1.5x over P3's TP1. That is about 0.6-0.9x of today's TP2 per request; aggregate per GPU stays above
  TP2 from C4.

## 5. Execution order and GPU plan (cap 8 gfx950)

- **Wave 1, in parallel:** WP1 (TP2, 2 GPUs), WP2 (2), WP3a + WP3b (2), WP4.1-4.2 (K2 split + GEMV family; 2-GPU rig).
- **Wave 2:** WP4 rest, WP5, WP6. WP3c starts after WP2 lands. WP7 starts after WP4.1 and WP2.
- **Integration:**
  - compose on one base;
  - re-test every reject;
  - search combinations;
  - run the full TP2 matrix at C1 / 2 / 4 / 8 / 16 (and C32 / C64 for information) plus TP1 at C1 / 4 / 16;
  - fresh server per arm-run, A-vs-A twin, rotated passes, medians, both nodes for every claimed win, and the accuracy
    gate on the final stack.
- **Early end-to-end check.** Every WP runs a quick served smoke at C1 / C8 in its own job before any isolated-kernel
  claim (Qwen lesson 5). Pack TP2 jobs 4 per 8-GPU node, and check `squeue` for our other rk-* jobs first.

## 6. Upside not booked
- Path A index-group residency (D0.1).
- Draft / verify overlap on disjoint XCDs.
- Rust direct backend wins carried in situ.
- `ar_dtype=fp8`.
- FP4 KV / index plane at long context. The workload here is short GSM8K; long context would move attention and the
  indexer up the bucket list.
