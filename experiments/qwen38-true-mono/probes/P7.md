(DESIGN 2.4 and 12.5 cells 2, 3, 3b, 10, 10b, 12, 12b, "derived; P7") rests on measured shares.

   (`r8g4`) layers 0.901 (target 0.858, draft 0.043), K=3 CONC 4 (`r16g4`) 0.927 (0.889 + 0.039); K=1 CONC 4 (`r8g2`)

5. **The served CONC 2-4 metric is diluted by prefill stalls: a decode gain shows at 0.8x (CONC 2) and 0.6x (CONC 4).**
   With the matrix's closed-loop client (refill on finish), every new request's prefill (ATOM's native eager path,

   decode-only round (this probe's trace method, or a no-refill client) next to served tok/s.

7. **Path A vs Path B matters less at wide builds.** The 47 in-graph boundaries are a fixed cost per layer inside the
   layer-kernel time; the wide round is 1.4-2.0x the `r4s` round, so G1 halves (about 0.012 hu at `r16g4`). Path B loses
   little there.
8. **Round mix at CONC 4 (closed loop, 32 prompts).** Decode time by G: G=4 0.80-0.86, G=1 0.04-0.11 (the drain tail),
   G=2 / G=3 0.005-0.10 each. Every refill prefill lands in a G = 3 round (`r12g4` / `r6g2`; at CONC 2 in a G = 1 round),
   so the G = 4 build is the one to optimise and the G = 3 builds matter mostly as prefill carriers.
9. **Smaller findings.** (a) ATOM's deferred output runs one uncounted target pass per request after its EOS step:
   0.5-0.9% of request-rounds (a full round per request at CONC 1: about 0.006-0.009 hu at K=1/K=3 CONC 1); on-device stop
