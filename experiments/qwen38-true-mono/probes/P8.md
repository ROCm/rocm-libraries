# P8: QSA stage shares at long context (gfx950)

DESIGN.md 8.2, probe P8: the QSA layer's stage shares at L 32K / 128K / max, from the single-layer bench with stage
stamps, to anchor the long-ISL budget. This file gives ratios, shares and counts only. Absolute times are in
`hackathon-prep/private-perf/qwen38-true-mono/probes/P8.md`.

Status: **done. Gate met** on hardware, with one named exception in the shared harness's tolerance (section 3, root-caused).
- **Bench.** The plain mono, `best` and hybrid ALL qsa builds were timed at 12 contexts from 512 to 262,136 tokens, for
  r4s (K=3 verify) and r1 (K=0 decode, drafter later steps). Shuffled rounds, two controls, and the delta / delta_ple
  layers in the same process.
- **Repeats.** 3 full runs on n01-09, plus a 6-context first run on n07-17. The run medians agree within 0.01; the two
  nodes agree within 0.014.
- **In-server cross-check.** A rocprofv3 round decomposition of the served K=3 CONC 1 path at GSM8K / 8K / 64K / 248K
  prompts: A, ALL, and a recompiled A-vs-A control, in one job on n07-17.
- **Bench vs serving.** The bench-based round model matches the in-server traced rounds within 0.004 at every length.

     shrink slightly with random-prompt routing.

3. **The long-ISL cells are not structurally at risk. Their margin is the short-L margin diluted by the round's
   growth.**
   - A saving S (hybrid units) that does not depend on L is worth S / g(L) of the long cell's round. The factor 1 / g
     is 0.952 / 0.912 / 0.867 for K=3 at 8K / 64K / 248K, and 0.973 / 0.935 / 0.911 for K=0.
   - With the Q chain at parity (a bitwise port), the DESIGN's bitwise central of 0.94 at short L becomes
     **0.943 / 0.945 / 0.948** at K=3 8K / 64K / 248K. DESIGN 12.5 has cell 13 at 0.97 (0.88-1.05) "at risk"; P8
     re-anchors it at about 0.945 bitwise.
   - The accuracy-gated non-QSA levers (about 0.124 of the 0.14 central saving, i.e. without N4's under-budget part)
     give about **0.882 / 0.887 / 0.892** before any long-L lever. Long-L levers come on top, from the pools in item 5.
   - The risk that remains is a **slower rocKE port of Q3 / Q4 at long L**. Q3 + Q4 are 0.12 of the round at max, so
     a 10% slower port costs about 0.012 there. WP2c's QSA parity gate must time the Q chain at 32K, 128K and max,
     not only at served L.
4. **Where the long-L time goes (ALL qsa r4s launch, critical-path shares):**
   - The Q chain (Q3-Q7) is 0.36 of the launch at 1K and 0.58 at max.
   - At max: Q3 0.192, Q4 0.115, Q5 0.133, Q6 0.018, Q7 0.123.
   - Growth from 1K to max splits Q3 0.51, Q4 0.26, Q5 0.13, Q7 0.10.
   - At r1 (K=0, drafter steps), Q4 carries 0.58-0.84 of the growth: the single-row two-level selection chain.
     Q4 is 0.20 of an ALL r1 launch from 64K on.
5. **Lever pools for WP7 (cell's own round at max, K=3):**

   | lever | pool | note |
   |---|---|---|
   | N10 `q3=mfma` | Q3 0.073 | Q3 span is 0.24 of the launch: wide and bandwidth-like |
   | exact Q4 chunk spread (`q4_chunks`) | Q4 0.049 | wait/span 0.20, tail/span 0.30: the final rank and the chunk tasks |
   | N4b split-KV | Q5-Q7 0.107 | flat in L above 2K: the 512-block budget caps it |

   - At 64K the pools are Q3 0.027, Q4 0.052, Q5-Q7 0.108.
   - DESIGN booked N4b + N10 at long L 0.030 (before x0.75) for cell 13. The N4b + N10 pools are 6x that at max
     and 4.5x at 64K, so the booking is conservative, not optimistic.
6. **Two exact (bitwise) levers found in the step structure:**
   - **Q4's two-level selection.** It costs a step when a row's blocks pass `FAST_CHUNK` (8,192 blocks = L 32,768).
     The ALL r1 launch grows 1.111 -> 1.248 between L 32K and 64K, with Q4's share 0.14 -> 0.20. r4s already pays it at
     L 32K (its row 3 has 8,193 blocks). A merged single-level path, or a chunk spread whose final rank overlaps the
     chunks, removes most of the step.
   - **Q3's small-context path** (at most one block per wave, contexts up to 2,048 blocks) is SLOWER at its top end
     than the batched path just above it: ALL r1 at L 8,192 is 1.118 of L 1K, but 1.099-1.111 at L 8,704-32,768
     (Q3 share 0.043 vs 0.022).
     - Switching to the batched path earlier is worth about 0.02 of an r1 qsa launch near L 8K, the path's upper
       end. L between 2K and 8K was not measured.
     - It is exact if the cached-score path gives the same bits, which `qsa_fast.py`'s docstring states.
7. **The hybrid's levers do not grow with L.**
   - ALL / mono for the same qsa launch is 0.926 at 1K and 0.953 at max for r4s, and 0.856 vs 0.909 for r1. The
     absolute saving is about constant.
   - Holds and NUMA act on the L-independent part. A true-mono lever on the L-independent part behaves the same way.
8. **Served matrix cells 13a / 13b overstate the growth.**
   - MATRIX's random-prompt rounds (rnd = TPOT x acceptance, 2 pairs) imply 1.111 / 1.197 of the short round for A.
   - The traced decode rounds of the same lengths give 1.084 / 1.133 (A) and 1.098 / 1.154 (ALL). The traced ALL / A
     is 0.870 at 64K and 0.874 at 248K, against MATRIX's 0.866 [0.853-0.880] and 0.844 [0.788-0.899].
   - Use the traced values as the long-ISL baseline. The 8K cells agree: model 1.041 (A) / 1.050 (ALL), traced
     1.044 / 1.052, served 1.047 / 1.063.

**The M2 case (root cause, `p8_diag_m2.py`):**
- The kernel's M1 low-rank MXFP8 pack differs from the golden's in 1 code: block 6 of row 2. The cause is the down
  GEMV's summation order rounding `bf16(low)` across an E4M3 code boundary.
- M2's mixed row, recomputed from the kernel's own pack, agrees with the kernel within 0.00018 L2. The whole
  0.0020 enters through that one code.
- This is the mono's known HC drift class (DESIGN 12.4, 0.002). It does not depend on context length. The
  harness's 1e-3 L2 bound is tighter than the mono's own drift. It held at L 639 / 8191 only by chance of data.
- The shared test file is unchanged. P8's wrapper applies the 2.5e-3 bound only in its second run, and only to the
  two `mixed_vs_hc_mono` checks.

**What ALL writes differently (`p8_diag_ss.py`, reported, not gated):**
- r4s: ALL leaves the MoE `dn` hand-off unwritten; the fused function keeps it on chip.
- r1 delta: ALL leaves B3's `core` hand-off unwritten.
- **r1 (qsa and delta): the rocKE fused MoE writes 14,080 pairs into row 0 of the FlyDSL `ss` region** (Q5 -> Q6
  scores) with tag word 0x80000088. These are its own staging words.
  - In a qsa layer this happens after Q6 has consumed `ss` (Q6 -> Q7 -> C1 -> M1 -> M2 -> M3 order), so outputs are
    bitwise.
  - The next launch's Q6 polls for a positive tag, so today it is harmless.
  - It is latent: the mono's int32 tags wrap after 2^25 counter steps, and the wrapped tag of slot 7 equals
    0x80000088. Rule for the true mono's layout (WP2): a fused function's private staging must not alias a live
    mailbox.

## Verification (independent verifier, 2026-10-30)

**Verdict: confirmed.** P8's gate (DESIGN 8.2: QSA stage shares at L 32K / 128K / max, anchoring the long-ISL budget)
was met on hardware. The numbers in sections 1-2 can be reproduced from the raw job outputs. No cluster rerun was
needed: every gate below is in the on-disk logs of jobs 68220260 / 68220573 / 68220752 / 68220907 / 68220685

What was checked:
- **Numerics ran on hardware, with a mutant that fails.**
  - full5 `num/numerics.jsonl` (n01-09, gfx950) records Q4 `selection_top512` / `selection_all` as bitwise in 59 of
    59 records. The strict run stops at the M2 case before 32K r4s row 3; `num_hc` has all 60 (12 L x 5 rows),
    including L 262,136 (65,534 blocks).
  - The Q4 mutant run fails 4 of 4 at `Q4.selection_top512`, with 512 of 512 indices differing.
    `p8_job.sh` requires every case to fail at exactly that check.
  - The bench-side checks hold in every run (122 checks per variant): the stale-table and per-L-distinct mutants, and
    every timed build bitwise against the plain mono at every L.
- **The superseded r1 failures are scratch-only.** I re-read full2 / full3 r1 (26 failing checks each): outputs, QSA
  caches, recurrent / conv states, determinism and NUMA copies are all bitwise. Only `ss` / `core` mailbox words
  differ. So the timing taken in those runs used outputs that were correct.
- **Timing discipline** (`p8_bench.py`):
  - 16 rounds per run, with a different seeded shuffle each round (16 of 16 orders distinct in every run).
  - Ratios are paired within each round.
  - Two controls: `mono2`, a second graph, and `mono_rc`, a recompiled build. Control bands, as I recomputed them:
    r4s [0.9939, 1.0050], r1 [0.9978, 1.0020].
  - Timing is unstamped; stamps are used only for shares. Node and GPU bus are logged per run.
  - Runs: seeds 1 / 2 / 4 on n01-09, plus n07-17 for cross-node.
  - In busy windows the sampled clocks are 2336-2405 MHz, so no throttling was seen. Note that the 9.1
    "drop throttled rounds" step is not implemented.
  - The win rule does not apply: P8 is a measurement probe and claims no lever win.
- **Numbers reproduced from the raw rounds** (median of the 3 n01-09 runs):
  - r4s, L / 1K at max: mono 1.474 and ALL 1.521. r1: ALL 1.342.

1. **The strict golden run exits rc=1 in every job, so job rc=1.** The single failure is the existing mono kernel's
