Repro kit: the non-repo inputs of the frozen stack (c2s3b / c2s0b), copied byte for byte from the frozen harness stage
h-qk-c2-f (sha256 ce005780d12f7bad..., the stage every measured c2 job ran). Paths under harness/ are the stage's own,
so kit/harness can be mounted as the harness stage and the measured scripts run unchanged. SHA256SUMS covers every
file. Nothing here holds absolute performance numbers.
