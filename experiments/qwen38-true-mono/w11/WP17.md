### How N2 is built
Each B3 sibling task (value head `vh`, split `s` of `S`) already holds the head's 128 f32 core rows after the sibling
exchange (the RMS needs them). It polls the head's other z values (B1 tiles, one more qkvz poll in the loader phase),
forms the gated output of all four 32-row blocks with exactly the stock epilogue's operations (so its own block's
pack equals the stock B3's publish bit for bit, and every sibling computes the same pack for the others), stages the
head's MXFP8 pack in LDS, and runs one scaled MFMA (16x16x128, MXFP8 x MXFP4: the head is one k_iter of C1's chain)
per 16-column tile of its share 160 / S of the output tiles (A row m = request row m / 4, so lane group g holds row g).
Each partial P[r][t][vh][c] goes to the region as a tagged pair. C1 task t polls the 48 heads' partials of its 16
columns (all rows), sums them in a fixed order, then runs the stock HC write.

## 3. Numerics (tolerance class; DESIGN 12.4 T0, T1)

Job `w17-n2c` (n07-17 GPU bus 101), 11 cells, every check per cell. T0 = the C1 output streams `w` (bf16) of the
base and of each arm against the fp64 golden `bf(h + bf(bf(proj) * inj))` computed from the BASE's own B3 pack and
the layer's out_proj weights (`synth_params` regenerated), aggregated over 8 random layer inputs. PASS = the arm's
max bf16-ulp error <= max(the base's, 1) and its RMS <= 1.25x the base's. `max_ulp` is dominated by near-zero values
(a 1-ulp f32 change can be thousands of bf16 ulps of a tiny number); it is the same for base and arm in every cell.

| cell | T0 RMS arm / base (`order=seq`) | `order=chain` | w differs from base (values / total, 8 inputs) | layer output equal to base (inputs) | partial max error / sum of abs terms | B3 pack equal | mutants fail T0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| delta r4s | 1.000 | 1.008 | 1 / 327,680 | 8 / 8 | 1.6e-4 | yes | 4 / 4 |
| delta r1 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0 / 81,920 | 8 / 8 | 7.8e-5 | yes | 4 / 4 |
| delta_ple r4s | 1.014 | 0.999 | 6 / 327,680 | 6 / 8 | 1.3e-4 | yes | 4 / 4 |
| delta_ple r1 | 1.012 | 1.012 | 3 / 81,920 | 7 / 8 | 6.9e-5 | yes | 4 / 4 |
| delta r2s, r3s, r2, r3, r4; delta_ple r2s, r3s (check only) | 1.000 | 0.994-1.000 | 0-1 | 7-8 / 8 | 7.7e-5-1.4e-4 | yes | 4 / 4 each |

- Determinism: N2 twice, layer output, states and the whole N2 region bitwise equal (11 / 11).
- Every partial pair carries the launch tag (11 / 11). The partial error bound (2^-11 of the |terms| sum) is the
  MFMA's own: the first job gated at 2^-16 (an f32 bound) and failed at 0.8-1.6e-4 in every cell while the C1 output
  was bitwise equal to the base, i.e. the hardware scaled-MFMA sum is not an f32-exact dot (alola rule 236). The
  mutants are 3-4 orders of magnitude above it.
- Mutants (each must FAIL T0, each did in 11 / 11 cells): `drop` (C1 leaves head 0 out) and `dup` (head 0 twice):
  T0 RMS 0.027-0.041 abs (vs 1e-4); `kit` (B3 uses head vh ^ 1's weights) 0.31-0.39; `ascale` (next block's A
  exponent) 0.19-0.28.
- `order=chain` (C1 adds the partials in the stock chain order, `((p0 + p1) + p2) + p3` of 12-head chains) is not
  bitwise to the stock C1 either (0-4 differing values per cell): the MFMA's accumulate is not an f32 add of the
  rounded per-head dot, so no fixed order of f32 partials reproduces the chain.
- T1 (layer, information): where a launch's layer output differed from the base, rel L2 4e-6-0.011 (a 1-ulp stream
  change that flips one bf16 rounding or one routed expert downstream; the MoE route is unchanged code). By
  construction the only changed stage is C1's sum, and its drift is inside T0.

## 8. Not built: the Q7 side (qsa layers)

Q7's output reaches C1 exactly as B3's does, but a Q7 task is (request row, kv head, 4 q heads, one 32-dim tile): 48
tasks per request row on 48 CTAs, rounds over the rows at r4s. Its N2 epilogue would multiply 128 activation values
(4 heads x 32 dims, four different out_proj k_iters) by the whole 2560-column slice: 160 KB of weights and 160 MFMAs
(20 per wave) per task, i.e. 4x the stock C1's per-CTA GEMV at r1 on 48 instead of 160 CTAs, and 4x the out_proj
weight bytes in total at r4s (each request row's tasks re-read the slice). The B3 form, with 40-80 KB and 40-80
MFMAs per CTA on 96-192 CTAs, already loses 2.3-6 % per layer by the producer-tail mechanism of section 5, so the Q7
form is dominated on both cells it would serve. It also needs a gathered B operand (32-K slices of four k_iters per
lane), whose per-lane K layout of the scaled MFMA is not established in this tree. Not built; recorded here instead
of silently dropped. The bitwise C1-streaming twin covers the qsa C1 (section 6: loses).
