## 1. Verdict

**Gate met.** The pinned hybrid's own A-vs-A is measured per suite and length on a fixed RULER mix; T4 tolerances and
the T2 budget B are set from planted perturbations; both gates provably fail on must-fail mutants and pass their
must-pass twins; every tool has unit tests and killed mutants.

- **Canonical RULER mix v1** (section 3): 7 NIAH/VT tasks, `max_gen_toks` 4096, fixed documents (prompt hashes equal
  in every run). The quick track's "0.65 vs 0.98" was the 256-token cap truncating the reasoning model, not retrieval.
- **T4 tolerances** (section 4), as a 2-sigma band of one run-to-run difference or the observed range, whichever is
  larger: GSM8K 0.012 (flexible) / 0.014 (strict), HumanEval 0.061, RULER 8K 0.012, 32K 0.015, 128K 0.031, max 0.044
  (at 350 / 350 / 175 / 175 samples per run; 0.017 / 0.021 at 175 for 8K / 32K).
- **B** (section 6): planted relative noise on every layer output stays inside every suite at 2^-4, fails at 2^-3
  (GSM8K, RULER 8K) and 2^-2 (everything), so B is the T2 drift of all:2^-5: **B = 7.81e-3 nats of mean per-token KL
  over the decode-forced gsm8k positions** of the canonical T2 protocol (section 5; 4 runs in 2 jobs on 2 nodes,
  7.74-7.94e-3). Reported alongside: 1.75e-3 on replies alone, 0.26 pp greedy flips.
- **T2 rule, rev 3** (section 5.5; replaced rev 2 in the fix round, section 12): a lever with share f, run n times
  beside k same-job reference runs, passes iff (a) the mean d of its gsm8k KL increments is <= f B + 2 sqrt(sd_ref^2 /
  k + S0^2 / n) (false-fail control; S0 = 4.06e-4, the pooled run-to-run sd), (b) d + 2.5 sqrt(sd_ref^2 / k +
  sd_arm^2 / n) <= 2 f B (the data exclude a lever at twice its share **with the lever's own spread**), (c) its runs
  spread no more than 3 S0, and (d) the job meets the share's design. Designs (`mc_gate.py`: a 2x-share lever passes
  <= 2.5 % at every arm sd from 1 to 8 S0): f 0.2 (N1, N3, N4) and f 0.15 (N2, N5) need k 4 / n 5, f 0.1 k 10 / n 8;
  f 0.05 (N6, N7 alone) has no design up to 60 runs: gate N6 + N7 together or at T5.
- **Two rules were replaced.** Rev 1 (one arm run against k references) failed its pre-registered test in `t2g2`:
  one of two runs planted at 2x N1's share passed (0.05e-3 under its limit). Rev 2 (arm mean, margin from S0 only)
  held its pre-registered predictions in `t2h2` / `t2i3` (section 5.6), but the review showed its miss rate grows with
  the lever's own spread (a 2x-share lever passes 9-36 % at an arm sd of 2-8 S0 at k 4 / n 2), and over-budget planted
  arms are in fact noisier than the references. Rev 3 adds the 2x-exclusion test, applies the spread test at every n,
  and sets the run counts by Monte Carlo; it was pre-registered (hash printed in each job log before the first run) and
  tested prospectively in `t2j` / `t2k` at its minimum design (section 5.7).
- **Must-fails** (section 7): all:2^-2 fails all T4 suites; all:2^-3 fails GSM8K and RULER 8K; under rev 2 planted arms
  at about 2x N1's share fail the N1 gate and at about 0.5x pass in 4 of 4 jobs (t2e, t2f, t2g2, prospective t2h2);
  arms named 2x / 0.5x N2's share fail / pass (prospective t2i3; measured 2.64x / 1.31x, not a true bracket); under rev 3, at its minimum
  design k 4 / n 5, `b2x` (2.20x) and `n2x2` (1.94x N2's share) fail and `bhalf` (0.72x) and `n2q` (0.87x) pass
  (prospective t2j / t2k); 13 of 13 hook mutants and 27 of 27 gate mutants killed.
- **Finding that shapes every served A/B:** the served path is not deterministic *inside one server process*: the
  same forced tokens sent twice differ from the first sample on (1 bf16 ulp in a 1-token prefill's logits), growing
  through decode to a per-token KL of about 7-8e-3 on replies. T2 is therefore statistical (floors from same-job
  reference runs), never bitwise; a deterministic prefill / decode (WP4h, Tier G) would cut T2's floor by about an
  order of magnitude and resolve the small shares.

## 2. What was built

**ATOM-truemono (default-off, host-side only; coordination note in section 10):**

- `ATOM_Q38_PERTURB=<scope>:<scale>[:<seed>]` (`atom/models/qwen4_exp_mono/q38_accuracy.py`): after each target layer
  launch in scope (`all`, a kind, or layer ids), every bf16 element `x` of the layer's output (the HC streams the next
  layer reads) becomes `bf16(x (1 + 2 s u))`, `u` in [-0.5, 0.5) a hash of `x`'s bits, its column, the layer and the
  seed: a function of the values (same input, same bits: DESIGN 12.1 rule 2), captured into the decode graphs with the
  layer launches (decode, verify and grouped-verify widths), the drafter untouched. The stand-in for a non-bitwise
  lever's drift and the must-fail mutant of the accuracy gates.
- `ATOM_Q38_TF=<dir>` (same module, called from `model_runner.postprocess`'s non-speculative branch): teacher forcing at
  K=0, one request at a time. The client names the request (`cur.json`); the hook forces generated position `g` to
  `golden[g]` and records per position the server's own greedy token, the forced token and its log-prob, the top-64
  ids / log-probs, and the log-probs at a reference record's top-64 ids. Records move to the host every 256 steps (a
  33K-position request ran the model process out of memory with per-step device tensors).
- `tests/test_qwen4_exp_mono_accuracy_hooks.py`: 21 CPU tests, run as `PRE_TESTS` in every hook job (21 passed on the
  cluster with the final module): off by default, scope / scale parsing, determinism, size (relative change within
  the scale plus one bf16 rounding, mean near 0, uniform spread), scale 0 = identity, zeros stay zero, independent
  noise per layer / seed; forcing only inside `golden`, prefill anchoring and chunk re-anchoring, steps of more than
  one request and requests first seen on a decode step untouched, reference gathers (incl. `ref_key`), host spilling.
- **Hook mutants** (`mutants_hooks.py`, one-site edits of a copy of the module; a mutant counts only when a test fails,
  pytest rc 1): **13 of 13 killed** (noise no-op, scale doubled, one-signed noise, salt ignoring the layer, scope
  ignored, forcing off, position off by one, no re-anchor, spill dropping a chunk, wrong reference row, forcing with
  two requests in a step, `ref_key` ignored, forced token recorded as the greedy one). `test_t2_stats.py` (3 tests)
  checks the KL coarsening against the full KL and a hand value, flips, and the position classes.

**Harness:** `tf_client.py` (record / force; `--prefill-tokens P`: only the prompt's first P tokens are prefilled, the
rest of the prompt is forced through the decode path; `ignore_eos` because the chat template's end-of-turn tokens are
part of the forced text; `--repeat` for within-process determinism; client bounded by `timeout`), `t2_stats.py` (KL of
the reference's top-64 + rest coarsening, a lower bound of the full KL; greedy flips against the reference's greedy
token; positions split into prompt / reply / after the reply's end token, the last excluded), `t2_gate.py` /
`t4_gate.py` (the rules of sections 4 and 5 as code), `acc_stats.py` (per-run accuracy, A-vs-A spread, per-sample
discordance, fixed-task-set check by prompt hash).

## 3. The canonical RULER mix (v1)

With `max_gen_toks` 256 the reasoning model often never reaches its answer (quick job qk-rulA, hybrid at 8K:
`niah_multiquery` 0.35 and `ruler_vt` 0.00 on documents that score 0.96-1.00 with a 1536 or 4096 cap), so a 256-cap mix
scores "did the reasoning end in time", and the two quick mixes differed in how many such tasks they held. Fixed mix
(`truemono-p11/ruler_mix.txt`):

| item | value |
|---|---|
| tasks | `niah_single_1` (noise haystack), `niah_single_2` (essay), `niah_multikey_1`, `niah_multikey_2`, `niah_multivalue`, `niah_multiquery`, `ruler_vt` (lm_eval 0.4.13 generators; the essay haystack is fetched by the generator) |
| generation | greedy, `max_gen_toks` 4096, lm_eval `local-chat-completions` + `--apply_chat_template` (the model's default reasoning) |
| serving | hybrid ALL env, K=3, `--max-model-len 262141` at every length |
| lengths: samples per task, concurrency | 8K: 50, CONC 4; 32K: 50, CONC 4; 128K: 25, CONC 2; max = 257024 tokens: 25, CONC 2 |
| fixed task set | `--limit` takes the first N documents of seeded generators: every run of every arm had identical prompt hashes, document for document (`acc_stats.py`, every job) |
| cost per hybrid run | 8K + 32K about 26 min; 128K about 41 min; max about 89 min (prefill-bound) |

Lever T4 runs use exactly these items; P11's sensitivity runs used 25 per task at 8K / 32K (the first 25 documents).
Empty replies (the whole 4096 cap spent, or an empty answer) are 0-10 per run at every length for the hybrid and are
scored wrong. `niah_multikey_1` is the weakest task at 128K / max.

**T4 rule (`t4_gate.py`):** S = max(2 sqrt(r / n), observed range of the pool), r the pool's paired discordance rate
and n the arm's samples per run. An arm passes a suite / length iff mean(arm runs) >= mean(pool) - S (one-sided).
The band (the binomial 2-sigma of one run-to-run difference) keeps S from shrinking with a lucky small pool; the range
keeps the DESIGN's literal "A-vs-A spread" when runs vary more than the binomial (GSM8K: one hybrid pair 3 sigma
apart). `p11ref` (the hybrid env on ATOM-truemono, hooks off) passes every suite against the hybrid pool (deltas
-0.004 / -0.006 GSM8K, -0.006 HumanEval, +0.003 / +0.004 RULER 8K / 32K).

## 5. T2: teacher-forced drift

### 5.1 Protocol (canonical, `p11_budget.json` "protocol")

K=0 server (r1 builds) with `ATOM_Q38_TF`; one **record** run (the reference: its tokens are the golden text), then
**>= 4 forced reference runs** of the same arm and the **arms**, shuffled, each a fresh server forced to the record.
`tf_client --prefill-tokens 1 --sets "gsm8k:128:512 humaneval:64:512"`: the prompt's first token is prefilled, every
later prompt token and up to 512 reply tokens go through the mono decode path (about 50K reply and 25K forced-prompt
positions per run, 10-15 min per run). **Gate metric `kl_gsm8k`:** mean per-token KL(reference || arm) over every
decode-forced position of the 128 gsm8k items (prompt text and reply up to its end token). Reported: reply-only KL
(all items), greedy flips, humaneval-set KL, prompt-position KL. B and the floor are protocol-specific.

### 5.2 The floor is the served path's own non-determinism

- Same forced tokens, two server starts: never bitwise equal (0 of 54 / 51 requests in every job).
- Same forced tokens twice **in one server** (job 68235332, `--repeat 2`): 0 of 51 bitwise equal, from position 0 on.
  Position 0 is a 1-token prefill whose top-2 log-probs are identical across requests while lower ranks move by one
  bf16 logit ulp (0.125); decode then amplifies it (long forced text: KL about 0.1 per token at 8K-32K contexts).
- With the whole prompt prefilled the prefill's own noise enters directly (position-0 log-probs move by up to about
  1 nat between starts); `--prefill-tokens 1` keeps the comparison on the decode path.
- Alignment checked (`t2_align.py`: greedy tokens match the golden token at offset 0, not +-1); the flips sit at
  near-ties (median top-1 / top-2 gap at a flip 0.125 = one logit ulp in the prefill-mode runs).

### 5.5 The T2 rule and per-lever limits (rev 3)

`t2_gate.py --rule mean` (the default; rev 3 since the fix round, section 12). A lever arm with share f of the drift
budget (DESIGN 12.4 split) is run n times beside k forced reference runs in one job (seeded shuffled order). With d =
the mean over the arm runs of kl_gsm8k minus the mean over the references, S0 = 4.06e-4 (the pooled within-group sd of
every replicated arm of `t2e`, `t2f`, `t2g2`: 17 dof), sd_ref = max(sd of the k references, S0) and sd_arm = max(sd of
the n arm runs, S0):

    within   d <= f B + 2 sqrt(sd_ref^2 / k + S0^2 / n)                 B = 7.81e-3
    excl2x   d + 2.5 sqrt(sd_ref^2 / k + sd_arm^2 / n) <= 2 f B
    quiet    sd(arm runs) <= 3 S0                                       (any n >= 2)
    PASS     iff within and excl2x and quiet and the job meets the share's design (k, n); below the design the
             verdict is UNRESOLVED (exit code 3), never a pass

`within` controls false fails: a lever at its share with the hybrid's spread passes, and the lever's own spread never
widens this limit. `excl2x` controls misses: the data must exclude a lever at twice its share, using the lever's own
spread, so a noisier lever needs more runs to pass, never fewer. `quiet` fails a lever noisier than the hybrid. The
designs come from a Monte Carlo of the rule's own model (`mc_gate.py`, refs ~ N(0, S0), arm ~ N(m f B, c S0)): the
smallest k + n with P(PASS | 2x share) <= 0.025 at every arm sd c from 1 to 8, P(PASS | 0.5x, c 1) >= 0.95 and
P(PASS | at the share, c 1) >= 0.90 (`results/mc_gate.txt`, 400K trials; `results/mc_gate_search.txt`, a second seed):

| lever | share f | f B | design k / n | P(PASS) 0.5x (c 1) | at the share (c 1) | 0.5x (c 2) | 2x, worst arm sd | 2x (c 2.6) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| N1, N3, N4 | 0.2 | 1.56e-3 | 4 / 5 | 1.000 | 0.979 | 0.936 | 0.018 (c 3.5) | 0.012 |
| N2, N5 | 0.15 | 1.17e-3 | 4 / 5 | 0.999 | 0.915 | 0.885 | 0.021 (c 3.5) | 0.018 |
| N6 + N7 together | 0.1 | 0.78e-3 | 10 / 8 (or 9 / 9) | 0.999 | 0.900 | 0.851 | 0.015 (c 2.6-3) | 0.015 |
| N6, N7 alone | 0.05 | 0.39e-3 | none up to k + n = 60 | - | - | - | - | - |
| N8 | 0 (output-exact) | - | - | - | - | - | - | not T2 (T3 acceptance) |
| T5 composed | 1.0 | 7.81e-3 | 4 / 5 (the 0.2 design covers larger shares) | - | - | - | - | - |

The same table for rev 2 at its old minimum designs: P(PASS | 2x share) rises from 0.011 (c 1) to 0.143 (c 2.6) and
0.361 (c 8) at f 0.2, k 4 / n 2, and to 0.135 at f 0.15, k 6 / n 3 (c 2.6-3). The price of rev 3 is runs: an f 0.2
or f 0.15 gate is 4 + 5 runs (about 2 GPU hours) instead of 4 + 2 or 6 + 3. Reply KL (B 1.75e-3, S0 1.28e-4), flips
(B 0.26 pp, S0 0.046 pp) and the humaneval-set KL (B 0.62e-2, S0 0.63e-3) are printed by `t2_gate.py` with the same
rule, as information. T2 runs at K=0: it exercises the r1 decode builds; a lever that only changes the verify (r4s)
builds is not exercised by T2 (section 9).

**Rev 2 and why it was replaced (fix round).** Rev 2 was `within` plus `quiet` at n >= 3 only, resolved iff
f B >= 4 S0 sqrt(1/k + 1/n). That resolution assumed the lever's run-to-run sd is S0. It is not, for the arms that
matter: the over-budget planted arms (`b2x`, `n2x`) have a pooled sd of 1.74 S0 (7 dof) against 1.03 S0 for the
references (19 dof), and `n2x` in `t2i3` had 2.6 S0. Since `within` ignores the arm's spread, the variance of the
arm's mean grows with it and rev 2 misses more (P11 section 11 item 3). `t2_gate.py --rule mean2` keeps rev 2 to
reproduce the `t2h2` / `t2i3` verdicts.

**Rev 1 and why it was replaced.** Rev 1 gated each arm run alone: d_run <= f B + 2 sd_ref sqrt(1 + 1/k) with the
job's own sd_ref, resolved iff f B > margin. That bounds false fails (an arm exactly at its share fails about 2.5 % of
runs) but not misses: a 2x-share arm's expected d exceeds the limit by only f B - margin, about one sigma in a job
with sd_ref 0.4e-3, so it passes about one run in six. `t2g2` tested rev 1 prospectively with pre-registered
predictions (`b2x` fails in both runs, `bhalf` passes both, `n2x` fails, `n2half` passes): `t2_10_b2x` passed
(+2.45e-3 against a limit of 2.50e-3); the other predictions held. `t2_gate.py --rule run` keeps rev 1 to reproduce
those verdicts.
