# P13: QSA under the index budget - dense order (N4a) and the fused split-KV softmax (N4b), in situ (gfx950)

DESIGN 12.2 probe P13 / D0 section 5 (wave 1a; precondition of WP14 = N4): when a request row's complete index blocks
fit the budget (`(L + 1) / 4 <= 512`, i.e. L + 1 <= 2048, the served GSM8K range), (a) does skipping the scoring /
selection (Q3 scores, Q4 select + rank, the `sel` hand-off) keep the outputs within tolerance, (b) does a fused split-KV
softmax (Q5 + Q6 + Q7 as one flash-decode stage) keep them within tolerance, and (c) what does it save per qsa layer
(r1, r4s) in situ. Gate: the tolerance ladder (DESIGN 12.4) with must-fail mutants; DESIGN 9.1 timing.
Ratios and hybrid units only. Absolute times and tok/s: `hackathon-prep/private-perf/qwen38-true-mono/wave1/P13.md`.

Status: **done. Gate met for N4a** (built in the FlyDSL copy, T0 + T1 + stress draws + mutants + ISA identity on
hardware; 9.1 timing on both rocke nodes; quick e2e on the current best stacks). **N4b: T0 met** by an
arithmetic-faithful emulation fed the kernel's own Q5-Q7 inputs, validated bitwise against the kernel's Q7 packs; it
is not built in a kernel, so its in-situ time is not measured here (WP14 builds it). T2 (teacher-forced KL, P11) and
T4 (RULER) are not run for either: P11 is still in progress, and both are WP14's gates per D0 section 5.

## 1. What D0 / WP14 get

1. **The order question (DESIGN 12.2 P13).** Q4's under-budget list IS rank-ordered: `qsa_fast._final` polls every
   score when `cb <= 512` and `_rank` sorts them (score, ties to the lower block). Q5-Q7 are NOT order-free in
   arithmetic: Q5 is (each list position's score is its own fma chain: bitwise through the two lists in 576 of 576
   stress rows), but Q6's denominator (256 strided partial sums of 9 positions, wave butterflies, wave order) and Q7's
   PV sums (per thread over positions `128 i + 16 w + s`) are f32 sums in list order. The bf16 rounding of the
   probabilities and of the attention output hides the order almost always. **So N4a is tolerance class, near
   bitwise** (stress, 576 under-budget rows, 32 fresh input / cache draws per cell, section 3.2):
   - Q6 probabilities differ in 6.5e-6 of entries (124 of about 19.0 M);
   - Q7's MXFP8 pack is bitwise in 490 of 576 rows (2.1e-4 of the pack words differ);
   - the layer output is bitwise in 161 of 224 under-budget launches (r1 89 of 96, r4s 72 of 128). A launch with one
     flipped pack code moves most of the layer output's bf16 elements by an ulp (C1's out-projection spreads it);
   - T0 drift vs the fp64 golden, dense / mono: at most 1.0001 (relative L2) and 1.0000 (max) in every row.
2. **Value in situ (`qdense=3` vs `best`, per qsa layer, unstamped, 40 shuffled rounds, two controls, 3 jobs on both
   nodes):** r1 **0.918-0.921**, r4s **0.918-0.925**, WIN 40/40 in every cell and job; on the tuned qsa base (the
   lookback picksF/G sched group) r1 0.923-0.943, r4s 0.937-0.944, also 40/40 WIN. Rig-projected hybrid units:
   K=3 0.025-0.027, K=0 0.023-0.024 on `best`; K=3 0.019-0.022, K=0 0.016-0.021 on the tuned base.
3. **Quick e2e on the current best stacks (n01-09, 3 passes):** K=3 finalF + `qdense=3` vs finalF: ms per round
   (median) **0.985** (3 of 3 passes lower: 0.980-0.988), tok/s per request (median) **1.023**, acceptance within run
   noise. K=0 tunedF + `qdense=3` vs tunedF: tok/s per request (median) **1.025** (3 of 3 passes: 1.023-1.028), ms per
   token 0.978. **In hybrid units: K=3 0.015 (ms-per-round median; 0.011 on the mean round), K=0 0.020.** The served
   K=3 gain is about 0.5-0.75x of the rig projection on the tuned base; K=0 matches it.
4. **N4b (fused split-KV softmax), emulated (section 5):** with f32 probabilities, splits of 32 / 64 / 128 positions
   and merges in fixed groups of 8, the Q7 pack's T0 drift is 0.995-1.000 (relative L2) and 0.896-1.000 (max) of the
   KERNEL mono's on all 18 rows: PASS. Before the pack the split-KV output is about 5,000x closer to the fp64 golden
   than the mono's (the mono's error there is its bf16 probabilities), so N4b moves 5.3-6.7 % of the MXFP8 codes away
   from the mono's while staying as close to the golden. It is tolerance class in the T2 sense (a real deviation from
   the served numerics, toward the golden); T2 / T4 decide it in WP14. Mutants (a split dropped, doubled, the merge
   without the `exp(m_s - M)` rescale) fail T0 at 4.5-23x the mono's drift.
5. **Booking for D0 / 12.3.** DESIGN booked N4 (a + b) at if-pass 0.03 / 0.03, incr 0.022 / 0.022 at p 0.75. N4a ALONE
   is now measured end to end at about **0.015 / 0.020** on the best stacks, near bitwise. Suggested: book N4a at its
   e2e value with p about 0.95 (T2 / T4 still to run) and keep N4b's share as the remainder (its pool, section 4.3, is
   after N4a). N4a is a FlyDSL-only edit (`ATOM_Q38_SCHED=qsa:qdense=3`), so it is **hybrid-adoptable** (DESIGN 2.6):
   if the hybrid ships it, the true mono's structure-only margin shrinks by the same amount (as P3's spread).
6. **For WP14 (rocKE port).** Port N4a as a WP2c Q-stage knob with the same three levels and the runtime per-row
   branch; keep Q3's completing-block key write (later steps' cached scores need it). Level 3's two removed hops are
   the measured win; level 1 alone gets about 90 % of it at r1 but about 60 % at r4s (section 4.1), where Q3's work
   is on the path.

## 5. N4b: the fused split-KV softmax, emulated (`p13_splitkv.py`)

Inputs: the kernel's own Q5-Q7 inputs dumped from job b (q heads, gate, the caches after the launch, the mono's
list) for the 18 under-budget rows of the num cells, plus the kernel's Q7 packs of the mono and of `qdense=3`.

- **The emulator reproduces the kernel bitwise.** The mono's Q5-Q7 arithmetic (sequential fma chains, `mexp_fma`,
  the strided partial sums, the wave butterflies in their order, `bf16(e * (1 / den))`, Q7's per-thread sums, slot
  butterfly and wave order, the output gate, the MXFP8 quantizer), run on the mono's list and on the position order,
  gives the kernel's packs **bitwise in 18 of 18 rows** for both.
- **Split-KV** (position order; per split of S positions the split max, `e = exp2((x - m_s) log2 e)` in f32 with no
  bf16 probability, the split sum as a wave butterfly, `o_s` per head and dim a sequential f32 fma; merge
  `M = max m_s`, `w_s = exp2((m_s - M) log2 e)`, `w_s l_s` and `w_s o_s` summed in fixed groups of 8, `o = O / L`):

| variant | T0 dense / kernel mono (rel L2) | (max) | T0 | codes equal to the kernel mono | pre-pack drift / mono's |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S 32, G 8 | 0.995-1.000 | 0.896-1.000 | 18 / 18 | 0.933-0.947 | about 2e-4 |
| S 64, G 8 | 0.995-1.000 | 0.896-1.000 | 18 / 18 | 0.933-0.947 | about 2e-4 |
| S 128, G 8 | 0.995-1.000 | 0.896-1.000 | 18 / 18 | 0.933-0.947 | about 2e-4 |
| mutant: split 0 dropped | 6.0-12.8 | 4.9-14.4 | 0 / 18 | 0.09-0.17 | - |
| mutant: split 0 doubled | 5.7-10.2 | 4.5-10.7 | 0 / 18 | 0.11-0.19 | - |
| mutant: no `w_s` rescale | 14.8-18.1 | 9.1-23.0 | 0 / 18 | 0.05-0.08 | - |

"Pre-pack drift" = the attention output before the gate vs the fp64 golden, relative L2: the mono's is about 1.7e-3
(its bf16 probabilities), split-KV's about 3e-7. The f32 fma of a probability and a bf16 value is emulated in f64 and
rounded once (a double rounding only where the f64 sum itself rounds; not modelled). Emulated on the CPU; the same
emulator reproduced the kernel's packs bitwise, so the hardware `exp2` did not matter at the pack level here.
